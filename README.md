Welcome to my personal blog repository. This space acts as a living archive where I record my experiences, articulate technical insights, and preserve my thoughts in written form.

🌐 **Live Site:** [blogs-ewanjohndennis.vercel.app](https://blogs-ewanjohndennis.vercel.app/)

---

## 🎯 Why This Blog Exists

I created this blog to build a persistent, written record of my journey as a developer. Designed with a clean **markdown aesthetic**, the repository acts as a simple file-based Content Management System—where every post is written as a `.md` file, version-controlled with Git, and automatically rendered to the live site via Vercel.

---

## 📚 Topics & Content

- **Internship Experiences** – Key takeaways, challenges, and growth from my time working in the industry.
- **Project Showcases** – Architectural walkthroughs and features of software projects I design and build.
- **Case Studies** – Deep dives into technical problems, debugging stories, and engineering trade-offs.
- **Thoughts & Essays** – Musings on technology, software development, open source, and continuous learning.
